## Short bio

I live in Bavaria, Germany. I'm studying electrical engineering and
information technology at the Hochschule München (University of Applied
Sciences), and I work at the DLR, the German Aerospace Center. Outside of
work and university I'm a hobbyist amateur radio operator with a particular
interest in CW.

## Experience

## Education
