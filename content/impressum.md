## Privacy

**What this site does.** It serves static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no
analytics, loads no scripts, fonts or images from third parties, and has no
comment system.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you
visit, GitHub's servers process technical data such as your IP address, the
time of the request and the page requested, and may keep it in server logs.
I have no access to these logs. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in
publishing this site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for details.

**Email.** If you reply by email, I receive your address and message and use
them only to answer you. I delete them when the conversation is over, unless
you ask me to keep them or the law requires it.

**Your rights.** You can ask me for access to, correction or deletion of your
data, and you can complain to a data protection authority. For Bavaria this
is the [Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht](https://www.lda.bayern.de/).
